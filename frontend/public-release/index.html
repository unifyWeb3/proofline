The acceptance gap

An agent saying “done” is not proof that the work satisfied the agreement.

Application success, an HTTP 200, or a delivered file can show that work happened. They do not establish that the work met the agreed standard.

Proofline makes the standard explicit, evaluates the evidence, and preserves the finalized decision as a receipt someone else can verify.

Without Proofline

  • Completion is claimed by the worker or agent
  • Application success may be mistaken for acceptance
  • The decision remains subjective and difficult to carry forward

With Proofline

  • The acceptance standard is defined first
  • Completed work is evaluated through GenLayer
  • A finalized receipt preserves the decision and its provenance
